## 2. Role Analysis

**Mission Alignment:**
As an Intern GEN-AI at Swiggy, your role significantly contributes to the company's mission by leveraging advanced analytics and artificial intelligence to empower both employees and management. By building robust data models and interactive dashboards, you ensure that HR teams have accurate insights into employee engagement, attrition rates, and hiring trends, enabling them to make informed decisions that enhance workforce satisfaction and productivity. Your AI-driven solutions help in analyzing employee feedback and predicting attrition risks, thereby improving the overall customer experience by retaining talent and supporting restaurants. This directly aligns with Swiggy's mission of delighting customers and empowering restaurants through innovative technology solutions.

### Responsibilities
- Build and maintain HR data models in dbt for attrition, hiring funnel, engagement, and headcount metrics
- Ensure a single, reliable source of truth for HR reporting and analytics across the organization
- Design interactive dashboards in Plotly Dash to provide actionable workforce insights for HR and leadership teams
- Develop AI-driven HR analytics solutions using text-to-SQL, RAG, and automated narrative generation for reports
- Apply NLP and machine learning techniques to analyze employee survey feedback and identify key themes and risks
- Build AI agents to support HRBPs with attrition risk analysis, talent review preparation, and workforce decision-making

### Requirements
- Good understanding of SQL and ability to work with complex data queries
- Comfortable using Python and libraries like pandas for data analysis and problem-solving
- Exposure to AI/LLM tools and APIs through academic projects, internships, hackathons, or personal work

### Preferred Qualifications
- Strong interest in using data and analytics to understand employee trends and business challenges

### Competencies
- Technical proficiency in SQL, Python, pandas
- Experience with AI/LLM tools and APIs
- Interest in data-driven solutions for business challenges
- Ability to develop HR data models using dbt
- Skills in creating interactive dashboards with Plotly Dash
- Knowledge of NLP and machine learning techniques

### Evaluation Criteria
- Experience working with complex SQL queries and Python libraries like pandas
- Demonstrated exposure to AI/LLM tools through projects or internships
- Interest and ability in developing HR data models and dashboards
- Competence in applying NLP and machine learning for employee analysis
